Privacy Policy – Chess: Revolution

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chess: Revolution ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

To provide online multiplayer gameplay, leaderboards, and ad-supported free access, we collect the following types of data:

  • Account & Profile Information: Email address, display name (username), and unique User ID (Firebase UID) when you register or sign in.
  • Gameplay & App Activity: Match history, ELO rating, in-game actions, and app interaction metrics.
  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interaction data collected automatically through third-party advertising SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate players and maintain user profiles and online multiplayer matchmaking.
  • To calculate ratings, prevent cheating, and maintain service stability.
  • To display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements that keep the game free to play.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal account credentials. However, we integrate trusted third-party service providers that process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used for secure account login, user profile storage, and real-time match synchronization. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements within the app. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), cookies, approximate location (derived from IP), and app interaction data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads and measure ad performance. (Google Privacy & Terms)

4. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account remains active.

5. Children's Privacy

Chess: Revolution is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and associated personal data at any time:

How to request account deletion:

  • In-App: Open the Profile / Settings screen inside Chess: Revolution and select the Delete Account option to permanently erase your authentication record and Firestore profile.
  • By Email: Send a deletion request from your registered email address to chessrevo.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request – Chess: Revolution". Your account and all associated gameplay records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: chessrevo.support@gmail.com
